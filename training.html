01 The Teacher Signal (verified replay)

The student never learns from scratch: it distills the verified, expert-labeled telemetry of the NSL-KDD intrusion benchmark (UNB CIC — the canonical, peer-reviewed successor to KDD Cup 99). Each row is frozen as an ALERT … | EARLY_WARNING … example, audited, and split deterministically before the model sees it.

  • ✓Download verified against canonical row count (125,973 train / 22,544 test)
  • ✓Feature schema locked: 41 telemetry attributes + verified label + hardness
  • ✓Class distribution matches the published NSL-KDD distribution
  • ✓Deterministic stratified split by label + row-content digest
  • ◌Leak gate (n-gram train/val) — runs inside every training launch
